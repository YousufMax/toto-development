Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your typical project delivery timelines?
Most custom marketing websites and brand design initiatives take between 4 to 6 weeks from kickoff to deployment. Complex custom web applications, SaaS dashboards, or full-scale lead generation engine setups typically range between 8 to 12 weeks with weekly sprint demos.
How does TOTO Development verify B2B lead databases?
We use a 3-layer validation process: live SMTP handshake verification, MX record validation, and human cross-checking against active business registries. This allows us to offer a strict guarantee of under 2% bounce rates on all delivered lists.
What modern tech stack do you build web platforms with?
Our core modern stack revolves around Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, Node.js, and PostgreSQL, deployed across modern edge infrastructure such as Vercel, AWS, or Cloudflare. We tailor frameworks to ensure maximal performance, security, and scalability.
Do you offer fixed-price quotes or hourly retainers?
We primarily operate on transparent, fixed-scope milestones so you have predictable budget requirements with zero hidden surprises. For ongoing growth, marketing, or maintenance, we offer dedicated monthly sprint retainers.
What happens after our product or campaign is launched?
Every build comes backed with 90 days of complimentary warranty coverage and active performance monitoring. We track user behavioral metrics, fix edge-case bugs immediately, and ensure your team is trained to manage the CMS or tooling easily.
