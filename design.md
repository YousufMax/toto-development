## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end luxury digital technology craftsmanship. Rooted in the visual precision of premier creative software studios, it synthesizes editorial typography with futuristic glassmorphism and atmospheric illumination.

### Personality & Audience
- **Tone:** Authoritative, bespoke, visionary, and hyper-refined.
- **Audience:** Enterprise leaders, forward-thinking tech founders, and tier-one venture executives seeking elite digital engineering and brand transformation.
- **Emotional Response:** Confidence, intrigue, and exclusivity fostered through dark cinematic contrast and vivid electric neon green radiance.

### Design Movement: Atmospheric Editorial Glassmorphism
The aesthetic couples deep obsidian backgrounds with high-voltage neon-infused green ambient lighting gradients. Elevated floating glass capsules, razor-thin translucent borders, and fluid pill-shaped controls establish an interface that feels both tactile and ethereal.

## Colors

The palette transforms the dark digital studio canvas by replacing traditional royal violet glow effects with vivid, high-energy botanic electric greens.

### Color Roles & Semantics
- **Primary (`#00FC43`):** The core vibrant neon-green tone applied to highlighted display typography, interactive focal points, active states, and glow sources.
- **Secondary (`#0A8A22`):** Deep forest green tint utilized for pill borders, glassy card rim highlights, and high-contrast structural fills.
- **Tertiary (`#FFD5AC`):** Warm pastel peach accent, introducing a sophisticated chromatic contrast for specialized highlights and status badges.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#6F7A6A` & `#08080C`):** Muted slate obsidian serving as the stage, absorbing surrounding light and providing deep contrast.
- **Text & Foreground:** Crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary typography, transitioning to muted silver (`#9E9EA7`) and slate (`#63636E`) for secondary copy.
- **Atmospheric Glows:** Diffuse radial blends of `rgba(0, 252, 67, 0.18)` through `rgba(10, 138, 34, 0.28)` drifting under key focal components.

## Typography

Typography acts as the main tension driver in the interface, contrasting traditional high-fashion editorial serif typefaces with sharp, functional sans-serif mechanics.

### Pairing & Editorial Accents
- **Serif Headlines:** `Playfair Display` drives primary narrative headings. To produce the signature editorial studio emphasis, italicize hero focus words (e.g., *digital solutions*, *businesses*) and render them in `#00FC43`.
- **Systematic Body:** `Inter` handles all analytical information, microcopy, body text, and UI controls, guaranteeing legibility across dense cards and pill navigations.
- **Letter Spacing:** Headlines utilize subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to bind serif terminals tightly together, while metadata labels adopt positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an expansive fluid grid, leveraging generous negative space to evoke an editorial portfolio exhibition.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Margins set to `margin` (3rem), gutters set to `gutter` (1.5rem), centering a max-width wrapper of 1280px.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid scaling with `2rem` outer padding and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Compact flow with `margin-mobile` (1.25rem) and `gutter-mobile` (1rem).

### Layout Patterns
- **Header Floating Track:** The primary navigation bar sits detached from the viewport ceiling, centered horizontally with fixed-width constraints or intrinsic pill sizing.
- **Card Matrix:** Feature sections utilize balanced 2-column or 3-column asymmetric cards with internal padding scaled via `space-md` through `space-lg`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque dropshadows; it relies on semi-transparent surface stacking, frosted backdrop filters, and neon radiance.

### Atmosphere & Floor Glows
- **Ambient Green Lighting:** Large radial gradients placed behind focal content:
  `background: radial-gradient(circle at 50% 100%, rgba(0, 252, 67, 0.22) 0%, rgba(10, 138, 34, 0.08) 45%, transparent 75%);`
- **Glassmorphism:** Navigation rails and cards use backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` to `blur(24px)`) combined with dark surface tints: `rgba(11, 11, 18, 0.65)`.

### Border Rims
Every elevated card and interactive capsule is bounded by a hairline stroke:
- **Default Rim:** `1px solid rgba(10, 138, 34, 0.12)`
- **Hover/Active Rim:** `1px solid rgba(10, 138, 34, 0.35)`
- **Luminous Glow:** `box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(0, 252, 67, 0.15);`

## Shapes

The design system embraces fluid organic curvature inspired by glass capsules and precision-molded lenses.

### Corner Curvature Rules
- **Pills (`roundedness: 3`):** Buttons, status badges, chip links, and the floating navigation dock all employ continuous full pill radii (`9999px` / `rounded-full`).
- **Feature Cards (`rounded-xl`):** Heavy containers, showcase tiles, and service modules feature softened `2rem` to `3rem` corner radii, ensuring cohesion with pill buttons nested inside.
- **Inner Controls:** Input fields and secondary dropdown elements mirror the capsule treatment with a minimum radius of `1rem`.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Full pill silhouette with solid `#00FC43` or a soft linear gradient from `#0A8A22` to `#00FC43`. Text is set in `#08080C` (semi-bold). Hover states emit a soft mint bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 252, 67, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Ghost:** Translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`), border `1px solid rgba(10, 138, 34, 0.25)`, text in pure white with trailing iconography (`↗`).

### Navigation Dock
- A floating pill bar pinned centrally near the top edge. Encased in frosted black glass (`rgba(11, 11, 18, 0.7)` with `blur(20px)`), outlined by `rgba(10, 138, 34, 0.18)`.
- Internal links use `Inter` 14px in muted slate, transitioning to pure white on hover. Features an integrated primary pill CTA on the right.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Indicator Pill:** Ultra-compact pill containing a glowing dot (`#00FC43` with `box-shadow: 0 0 8px #00FC43`) paired with uppercase or capitalized title text (`label-sm`).
- **Service Tags:** Contained within `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` pill outlines with `0.5rem` vertical and `1rem` horizontal padding.

### Feature & Service Cards
- Dark glass containers layered over atmospheric neon lighting.
- Top or corner areas feature iridescent 3D crystal geometries or dynamic renders.
- Card titles utilize `headline-sm` or `title-lg`, supported by muted gray body descriptions and anchored with horizontal tag pill groups.

### Input Fields
- Dark translucent glass pill capsules (`background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`).
- Focused state transitions the border to `rgba(10, 138, 34, 0.5)` with an inner/outer neon mint glow.
- Placeholders set in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`.
