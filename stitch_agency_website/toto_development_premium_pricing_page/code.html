Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our fee structure, scoping, and collaboration process.

How are initial project costs estimated?
Our starting figures reflect standard scope deployments. During our 30-minute discovery call, we unpack your design requirements, third-party integrations, custom backend logic, and compliance rules to produce an exact, fixed-cost proposal with no hidden surprises.
Can we customize features between different tiers?
Yes, absolutely. The packages act as proven frameworks. If you require the Digital Launch suite with custom ERP integrations or specialized mobile app bridges, we add modular add-ons rather than forcing you into an unneeded enterprise tier.
What are your typical payment schedules and milestones?
Standard projects are typically broken down into milestone-based schedules: 40% initial commitment upon kickoff, 30% upon approval of UX/UI prototypes and core system staging, and 30% upon final testing, deployment, and handover.
How long does delivery take from kickoff to launch?
Digital Launch Suite projects typically ship in 3–5 weeks. Business Growth systems take between 6–10 weeks depending on custom feature complexity. Enterprise pipelines are organized into continuous agile sprints with bi-weekly staging deliverables.
Who owns the intellectual property and code?
You do 100%. Upon completion of payment milestones, all repositories, design source files, database assets, and codebases are transferred directly to your organization. No vendor lock-in, ever.
